Ensembles & blend

GEFS – NOAA/NCEP Global Ensemble Forecast System, 31 members (control + 30). ECMWF IFS ENS – 51 members (the IFS control, which is the HRES run, plus 50 perturbed members). Both are used at 0.25° on the same cycle and valid times.

Blend – the ensemble mean is the equal-weight average of the two family means (each system counts once; configurable to pooled-member weighting). The member minimum is the lowest value over all members of both systems. L markers are the low centres of every member of both systems, coloured by system; in the minimum product each L is credited to the member that supplies the lowest value there. The cursor read-out lists each system's value and the difference.

ECMWF's 06/18Z runs only reach 6 days, so on such a cycle the ECMWF and blend views end at F144.
